Help and support

Marmalade the cat has pinched Gnorman's fishing rod, so the little pottery gnome straps on a leaf blower and gives chase through the neighbours' gardens. Hold to fly up, let go to drop, and dodge the sprinklers, magpies and washing lines without cracking.

Questions and problems

Stuck, found a bug, or just want to say hello? Email fernhollowgame@gmail.com. Telling us your phone model helps.

Common questions

I bought Remove Ads but I'm seeing break ads. In the game, open the Shop → Seeds tab and tap Restore purchases. Optional reward videos stay, because you only see them if you choose to.

I got a new phone. Can I keep my progress? Your progress is saved on your phone and comes across when you restore your phone from a backup.

How do I see the lesson again? Open Settings (or pause during a run) and tap How to play.

How do I change my ad choices? In the game, open Settings → Ad privacy choices. On iPhone you can also go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and on Android to Settings → Privacy → Ads.
