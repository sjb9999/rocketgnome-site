Rocket Gnome
A one-finger flying game by Littlehollow Games

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

The short version. Rocket Gnome has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or anything like it. Your progress stays on your own device. The phone app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some information about your device to show and measure those ads, and in the UK, EU and Switzerland it asks your permission first. If you buy anything in the game, Apple (on iPhone) or Google Play (on Android) handles the payment.

Who makes Rocket Gnome

Rocket Gnome ("the game") is made by Littlehollow Games, an independent developer, called "we" on this page. If you have any question about this policy or your information, email fernhollowgame@gmail.com.

Your game progress

Your seeds, best distances, records, trophies, missions, outfits, blowers, upgrades and settings are saved on your device only. On a phone, they may also be included in your phone's own backups (for example iCloud or Google's Android backup), which we can't see. We have no servers that receive your progress, and deleting the app deletes it.

Ads in the phone app (Google AdMob)

The game is free because it shows ads: a short break ad now and then between runs, and optional video ads you can choose to watch for a reward (to keep going after a crash, to double your seeds, or for a free Head Start). These ads come from Google AdMob. To show them, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier: on iPhone only if you allow tracking when Apple's "Allow tracking?" message appears; on Android, unless you've deleted it in your phone's settings
  • your IP address, and a rough location worked out from it (country or city, never your precise location)
  • your device type, operating system and app version
  • which ads were shown and whether they were watched or tapped
  • diagnostic information, such as how quickly ads load

Google uses this to show ads, count them, stop fraud and, only where you've given permission, choose ads that suit you. You can read how in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices:

  • In the UK, the EU and Switzerland, a message from Google asks for your consent before any personal data is used for ads. You can change your answer at any time in the game's Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • On iPhone, you can change your tracking choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, you can delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (on some phones, Settings → Google → Ads).
  • If you say no, you'll still see ads, but they won't be based on your personal data.

We ask Google for ads suitable for all ages only.

Purchases

The phone app offers optional purchases: Remove Ads (which switches off the break ads), bags of seeds and a one-off starter pack. On iPhone, Apple handles the payment through the App Store; on Android, Google handles it through Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. The game only checks with Apple or Google what has been bought, so it can give it to you (and give Remove Ads back after "Restore purchases" on a new phone). See Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Playing in a web browser

When you play Rocket Gnome on a games website such as Poki, that website shows the ads and its own privacy policy applies to them. The game itself saves your progress only in your browser's storage on your device.

Children

Rocket Gnome is made for a general audience and isn't aimed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. If you think a child's information has been shared with us, email us and we'll help.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete personal information about you. As we don't keep any personal information ourselves, there's normally nothing for us to hand over, but we're happy to answer questions. For ad data, use the choices above or contact Google. If you're unhappy with how your information is handled, you can complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, or to the data protection authority where you live.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your information (for example a new feature or ad partner), we'll update this page and the date at the top.
